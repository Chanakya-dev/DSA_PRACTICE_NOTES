## Important advice

For your Java DSA preparation, prioritize these three areas:

1. Linear Search — master basic traversal and conditions.
2. Binary Search and its variations — learn boundaries, duplicates, insertion positions, and rotated arrays.
3. Binary Search on Answer — learn to identify a monotonic condition and use it to find the optimal answer.

You do not need to master every specialized searching algorithm before moving forward. Get strong at Binary Search and its variations first; they offer the most reusable problem-solving patterns.
